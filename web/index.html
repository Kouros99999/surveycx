Closed loop

A low score opens an Amazon Connect Task with the answers, the comment and a link to the contact, routed to the queue you choose. The scores are written back onto the contact as attributes, an event goes to EventBridge for Slack, your CRM or your warehouse, and Arena receives the agent's score.

What else does it cost?
The stack's own AWS resources at normal rates, a few dollars a month, plus SMS and email at AWS's per-message prices.
Does it work with Genesys or other platforms?
Yes. Any platform or middleware can report finished contacts through the ingest endpoint and SurveyCX sends the invitations and hosts the survey; answers collected elsewhere can be posted in too. See other platforms.
Does it feed Arena?
Each agent-attributable score goes to Arena for Amazon Connect as a survey score, through its ingest endpoint or the contact attribute Arena already reads.
Where does our data go?
Nowhere. SurveyCX runs in your AWS account. See security and data.

Questions people ask

How is the survey offered on a call?

Through a flow module in the disconnect flow: when the agent hangs up and the customer is still on the line, the survey plays and the customer answers on the keypad. No transfer, no second call.

What about chat?

The same module runs in chat: the questions are sent as messages and the customer replies with a number, or types a comment for the open question.

Can customers leave a spoken comment on the IVR?

Not yet. Free-text questions are skipped on voice and asked on every other channel. Spoken comments with transcription are on the roadmap.

Can we use our own identity provider?

Yes. Federate it to the user pool the stack creates, or launch with your provider's issuer and audience.

Is the web survey accessible?

It is built to WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard operable, labelled controls, visible focus, no colour-only meaning, and no grid questions.
